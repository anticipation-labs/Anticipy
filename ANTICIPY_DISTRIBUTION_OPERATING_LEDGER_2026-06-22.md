## North Star

Anticipy should not be marketed as "an AI pendant" or "another assistant app."

The marketable truth is:

> Anticipy hears one passing sentence and quietly handles the thing before it turns into work.

The product promise must always land as proof:

1. A real-world sentence happened in a normal day.
2. Anticipy understood the obligation, person, timing, and risk.
3. It prepared or completed the right action.
4. It asked only when the action touched another person, money, identity, permissions, or irreversible state.
5. It communicated like a sharp human assistant, not a task system.
