0The scale — read this before anything else

1What we're building

One paragraph, then the organism.

Anticipy is an ambient chief of staff. A pendant and an iPhone hear the owner's day; a server brain judges every utterance — stay silent, ask one question, or quietly start work; a memory graph keeps what matters with provenance; a confirmation gate holds anything irreversible for one tap; browser hands do the work. The product's entire value is trust under silence: it earns its keep in the ~5 moments a day it catches something real, and it destroys itself in any one moment it interrupts wrongly, invents a fact, or acts on someone else's words.

The organism — seven organs, each with one job

  • Ears — capture speech with real timestamps, on-device where possible. Decision quality is capped by ear quality forever; no downstream intelligence recovers a word that was never heard.
  • Name tags — know who spoke. Without attribution, the other person's words become the owner's commitments. Attribution is a precondition for memory writes and actions, not a feature.
  • The judge — a model, never a rule, decides what an utterance means: ignore / ask / act / answer, who was addressed, who owes what, and what the goal touches (compute / read / world). Cheap model sifts everything; a frontier model re-judges anything about to become an action.
  • Memory — episodes → entities → facts, each fact carrying importance and confidence and a traceable source quote. Remembering is unconditional; acting on memory is gated. Old facts age; contradicted facts are superseded, never silently overwritten.
  • The seatbelt — a deterministic gate below the model: anything that sends, buys, books, posts, or deletes waits for the owner's tap, bound to the exact version of the plan approved. The model cannot talk its way past it, and neither can text she overheard.
  • Hands — the browser agent, with an intent journal written before every click, so "did the send actually happen?" is answerable after any crash.
  • The mouth — every outgoing word is checked against provenance: a name, number, or date she was never given cannot leave the building. Templates over inventions, silence over spam, one digest after a conversation instead of four texts during it.

2Done — the definition that cannot be gamed

Done is when she's forgettable and nothing falls. A cold stranger — not the founder — onboards on their own accounts with zero hand-holding, lives a normal unscripted week, and: she caught real things they would have dropped, never once made them say "what?", never embarrassed them, never made them check on her. At the end of the week they don't want to give it back. Unburdened. Caught. Safe enough to forget it's there.

Why this survives the scale problem

The test set is next week's life, which hasn't happened yet. It cannot be overfit, memorized, or gamed by anyone — including the builder. Every transcript, gate, and eval in this brief is a thermometer: it tells you where she is sick. Only lived days can say she is well.

The daily metronome

One question every morning: was yesterday clean? Clean = zero corrections, zero confused replies, zero apologies-for-her — and at least one thing caught. Not clean → fix the one thing that broke it, then stop. Clean → touch nothing. The correction signal already exists in the data (the owner's own "What." texts, cancels, overrides); a nightly counter and a streak number is the entire dashboard.

Two rules with no exceptions

  • No session, agent, or green scoreboard may ever declare the product done. Only the calendar declares done — clean days, accumulating, on a stranger's real life.
  • Nothing counts as fixed until verified against the LIVE system — deployed, fingerprint-matched, observed. Repo-green is a claim, not a fact.

The definition, fifty times over

No abstractions: fifty moments from a real life and exactly what she does in each one when the product is finished. Every organ, law, and screen in this brief exists to make these fifty boringly true — and nothing is done while any of them is false.

3The laws — binding on whoever builds this

The full text lives at HARNESS-LAWS.md in the repo root, auto-loaded by every agent. The compressed six:

1 · No pattern-match may decide meaning.

No regex, verb list, word count, or string check decides what a human's words mean or what to do about them. Meaning belongs to a model with full context. Pattern-matching is legal in exactly three places: senses (audio plumbing), the seatbelt (what a plan touches), and deterministic tests.

2 · Tape ships only with an expiry.

An emergency string-level patch carries a TAPE: comment naming the real fix and a red gate leg tracking its removal. Tape without an expiry is a rejected diff.

3 · Nothing is fixed until its check is green against the LIVE system.

Production has served stale code while everyone believed otherwise. Every deploy is followed by a byte-or-behavior proof against the live service.

4 · State lives in files, never in conversations.

Plans, findings, and decisions go into the repo the day they exist. A conclusion that lives only in a chat will be re-derived — wrong — by the next session.

5 · The fix order is fixed: senses → context → examples → model tier → structure.

Before writing any rule, ask in order: is she deaf? blind? untaught? under-modeled? A rule written while any of those is unfixed is tape by definition.

6 · The owner is not the review loop.

Nothing ships until an adversarial pass has tried to kill it. The owner catching what you could have caught is a process failure. Self-review to convergence, then ship.

Pendant pairing & truthful BLE/transcription boundary (BLE/PendantManager.swift, Audio/LocalTranscriber.swift, BLE policies)partial

PendantManager implements the verified BLE transport: service 19B10000-E8F2-537E-4F6C-D104768A1214, Opus audio notifications on 19B10001 with three-byte packet headers and OpusFrameAssembler, codec id 20 on 19B10002, and standard battery service 0x180F/2A19. The central is created lazily for a person who chooses Pair a pendant, state restoration and saved-UUID reconnect exist, and the Settings → Connectors → Pendant page shows device, battery, signal, pair, and forget controls.

Pendant transcription is not implemented. The old TranscriberClient vendor-websocket path was deleted. AnticipySession.startPendantTranscription always clears pendant.onOpusFrame, retains only onGap lost-airtime diagnostics, and sets pendantCapturing=false. The pendant supplies Opus frames, while LocalTranscriber accepts decoded PCM; the iOS target has no Opus decoder joining those two pieces, so LocalTranscriber has zero working pendant call sites. Therefore a connected pendant currently produces no recording, transcript, event, or upload. Both Home and the connector page say this plainly and direct the owner to the working phone microphone. The missing local Opus decode/PCM bridge must be built before any “listening” claim is permitted.

8Proof machinery — thermometers, not finish lines

  • overnight/done_gate.py — the one scoreboard. Every work session starts by running it and works only its first failing leg. Its final leg cannot be faked: a real stranger's real signed day.
  • overnight/tejas_gate.py — 8 deterministic legs that execute the real organs against the worst recorded call. Regression tripwire for that failure class.
  • overnight/triage_eval.py — 25 labeled rows scored per-label on the production model (per-label is mandatory: silence must not be able to max the score). Current: 80%.
  • overnight/replay_call.py — replays a recorded call through the current brain on the recording's own clock. The strongest demo and the strongest tripwire we own.
  • tests/ — 1004 deterministic tests, zero network. Many are source-inspection tests that pin exact guards in place; do not "clean them up."
  • The clean-day counter (to build): nightly job stamping each day CLEAN or NOT from correction signals already in the data. The streak is the product dashboard — one number.

9Open problems — the honest list

  • Capture is the floor. ~67% word loss measured on a real call; no VAD; dictation-grade recognizer used for ambient audio. The designed fix (segments, capture-time keying, SpeechAnalyzer migration) exists on paper in CAPTURE-ARCHITECTURE.md — Steps 2–5 are unbuilt.

10Never build

  • Always-streaming pendant firmware (or a camera). Radio dominates the wearable power budget; wake-on-sound duty cycling is the field's proven shape. Refuse streaming-first.
  • Autonomous outbound beyond the current posture. Nobody in the field ships autonomous action from ambient speech. Every unsolicited message spends trust that refills slowly; the middle answer (ask) and the digest exist so she never has to guess out loud.
  • Cloud voiceprints. Understanding happens on-device; voiceprints and raw audio never leave the phone. This is law, not preference.
  • Fine-tuning / post-training now. The model was never the bottleneck; context, examples, and tiering were. Training is a later move that requires the eval flywheel this brief only begins.
  • A rewrite. The approval gate, the hands' intent journal, and memory provenance grade ahead of everything shipped in 2026. Burn nothing; fix the named organs.
  • Any new pattern-match on meaning. See Law 1. The three months this codebase lost to duct tape are the reason this document exists.
