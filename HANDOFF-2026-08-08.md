## 1. THE ONE NUMBER, and the only thing that matters right now

**Roughly one real conversation in four ends in total silence.** Not a wrong
card, not a half-message — nothing at all. No text, no card, no trace.

Omar's words: *"If you told me that a product has a 25% chance of failing, it's

### The root cause is isolated. Same conversation, two runs:

```
FAILING   ask    addressee=self    goal=Book Earls Brooklyn for Saturday at 1pm for owner and friend
PASSING   ignore addressee=person  goal=Book Earls Brooklyn for Saturday at 1 PM for 2 people
```

**The goals are identical.** She understands the plan perfectly in both runs.
The only thing that moves is the one-word `addressee` label. When it lands on
`self`, the self-talk rule in `anticipy_core.py` (search: `self-talk question
stays unasked`) swallows every question, no card is ever built, and the whole
plan is deleted.

**A wobbly one-word label is allowed to destroy a plan she fully understood.**
That is the defect. Fix that and the 25% goes away.

### What I was about to do when Omar stopped me

1. Run `proof/silence_rate.py 8` for a real baseline. **NEVER RUN — the number
   above is inferred from scattered batches, not measured properly.** Do this
   first; everything else is guesswork without it.
2. Candidate fix, untested: add `"self"` to `AMBIENT_ADDRESSEES` in
   `orchestrator.py`. Rationale — in passing runs the `person` label routes
   through the ambient lane, whose `_queue_job` dedupe is what keeps one plan
   to ONE card and ONE text. Self-classified plans never reach that lane, so
   they hit the plain path and get silenced instead. Routing them the same way
   should give identical treatment without touching the silence rule at all.
3. **DO NOT instead narrow the self-talk guard.** I tried exactly that this
   morning (allow the question when a goal exists) and it produced FOUR texts
   for one dinner and SIX for the Earls plan. Each turn of one conversation
   produces a slightly different goal, so the goal-keyed dedupe does not see
   them as the same thing. Reverted; a test now fails if it is re-attempted.
   See `tests/test_self_talk_question.py`.

### How to measure anything at all

```bash

```

**Run proofs SEQUENTIALLY, never in parallel.** Parallel batches share an
upstream routing window and fail together; a 6-of-6 green streak can be nearly
one sample. This is what made me blame two of my own changes wrongly.
