PART A — THE PRODUCT OMAR WANTS ("done"), IN HIS WORDS, MICRO DETAIL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Anticipy is a **proactive personal assistant — "Donna from Suits"** — that **listens to everything**
a person says/does and **automatically gets up to ~75% of their work done**, for ANYONE: founder,
lawyer, doctor, executive. Works on everything and anything. NO artificial limits (not "3 a day").

THE FULL FLOW (this is the product, front to back):
1) **ONBOARDING (in the Anticipy app):**
   - Asks the user a couple of questions; walks them through setup + installing the **Chrome extension**.
   - Then **opens the user's OWN already-logged-in Chrome** (their real LinkedIn, Gmail, every big site).
   - **Crawls site to site** — "if it finds one thing, it goes to the next thing" — using **custom tools
     for every little thing / every site**. It **scrapes EVERYTHING about you** and **builds a complete
     personal profile** — understands who you are, very specific to that exact person.
   - **Authorizes the user's specific apps** (Outlook, Google Calendar, AND their exact vertical app —
     e.g. **Cosmolex** for a lawyer's CRM) and **stands up a per-person API** for each.
   - Then it **jumps on a PHONE CALL with the user** to clarify whatever it's unsure about ("can I ask a
     couple questions?"). The user can then **add more input.**
2) **MAIN PAGE — input doors (all feed the same engine):**
   - **Start Listening** = the real always-on **Anticipy device**, actively listening and taking action.
     **THIS IS THE BIG ONE.**
   - **Type a transcript** / **Upload a transcript** = mainly for yourself (a full day of speech transcribed).
   - **Upload an MP3** = audio of a full day.
   - ⚠️ DO NOT GET BOGGED DOWN testing MP3/transcription/listening plumbing — "they just need to work."
     Do not get held up on the easy stuff and never solve the hard core.
3) **THE HARD CORE (where the real work is):** the **PROACTIVE ENGINE + ACTION ENGINE** that
   **understands INTENT and detects everything**, hears EVERYTHING and **automatically gets it done**:
   - "do the accounting" / "get X clients" → it does it.
   - "let's meet at 3" / "I'll get this over by 4" / wife says "pick up the kids at 3" / a text says
     "I'll remind you" → it handles all of it.
4) **TWO ARMS (internal):**
   - **BROWSER ARM** = browser-use, acting inside the user's own Chrome (everything without an API).
   - **API ARM** = **per-person**, very specific: the apps authorized at onboarding (Outlook, Calendar,
     their exact CRM like Cosmolex) — one API set per person.
5) **CONSTRAINTS / LAWS:** money/payment is the **only hard stop**. **Acting on a vent/sarcasm is the
   cardinal sin** (must be ZERO). Build it **fully autonomously**.

"DONE" (the finish line, Omar's words): for **5 real days**, Omar lives his actual day, Anticipy hears it,
catches the real tasks (incl. unspoken/indirect), does them for real **with receipts he can see** (calendar
event exists, draft written, the **2:45 reminder call actually rings his phone**), **never acts on a vent**,
and **he trusts it**. Strangers/onboarding-for-others/pendant come AFTER.
