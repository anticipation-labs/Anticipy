Three pieces. One assistant.

Everything runs on your accounts, in your browser, with your confirmation before anything irreversible.

01 — EARS

The pendant

A featherweight wearable that streams what it hears to your phone over Bluetooth. All-day battery. No screen, no buttons, no fuss.

02 — BRAIN

The app

Transcribes your day — in the cloud or fully on-device — and decides what matters: ignore the small talk, act on the commitments, ask when it's unsure.

03 — HANDS

The browser agent

A Chrome extension that works inside your logged-in sessions. Gmail, Calendar, reservations, portals — it does the clicking, you give the final yes.

Install the browser agent

Developer preview — loads straight into Chrome in under a minute. No store, no wait.

Download the extension

Grab anticipy-extension.zip and unzip it anywhere you like — Desktop is fine.

Open Chrome's extension page

Type chrome://extensions in the address bar, then flip on Developer mode (top-right toggle).

Load it

Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped anticipy folder. The green Anticipy dot appears in your toolbar.

Pair it

Click the Anticipy icon and enter the 6-digit code from your phone app. That's it — your assistant has hands.
