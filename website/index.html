Three pieces. One assistant.

Everything runs on your accounts, in your browser, and nothing consequential happens before you've said yes.

  • 01 — Ears

    Your phone

    The phone in your pocket is the microphone. It turns what it hears into text, and that text is what I work from.

  • 02 — Judgement

    The app

    Most of what you say is small talk and I let it go. When something is a commitment, I get it ready and bring it to you — in the app, or by text.

  • 03 — Hands

    The browser agent

    A Chrome extension that works inside your logged-in sessions. Gmail, Calendar, reservations, portals. I do the clicking; you gave the yes.

Install the browser agent

It's a developer install for now, and I'd rather you knew that before you start than after.

  • About five minutes, on a Mac or PC, in Chrome. It can't be done from a phone.
  • You download a folder and keep it. Chrome reads it from where it sits, so moving or deleting it stops me working.
  • It won't update itself. When there's a new version I'll tell you.
  • While I'm working, Chrome puts a yellow strip across your tabs saying “Anticipy started debugging this browser.” That's Chrome being honest that something other than you is clicking. It goes when the task is done.

The pendant

A piece of jewellery that happens to be a microphone, so your day is heard without your phone being out.

Straight answer on where it is: the hardware exists and connects over Bluetooth, but what it hears doesn't reach me yet — that path isn't finished. Until it is, your phone is the microphone, and everything on this page works without a pendant.

  • Wearing it

    Worn, not operated

    Nothing to read, nothing to press. You put it on and forget it — that's the whole point of it not being a phone.

  • What I keep

    Words, not recordings

    What you say becomes text, and the text is what comes to me so I can act on it.

  • Control

    Nothing without a yes

    Sends, bookings, cancellations — I prepare the whole thing and ask before any of it is real.
