# Anticipy Social-Network Card Reel Design

## Style Prompt

Native vertical short-form proof video for Anticipy, inspired by cinematic startup mythology but built from real desk/card/device assets. It should feel like a founder found the clean sentence for proactive AI, not like a polished SaaS explainer. First frame is a provocative line over a real card shot. The product reveal is delayed until the viewer understands the problem: prompt-bound AI waits, Anticipy catches life admin before the prompt.

## Colors

- Background charcoal: `#11100D`
- Ink black: `#191713`
- Cream: `#F4EFE7`
- Soft brass: `#C8A86A`
- Muted brass: `#8F7A4E`
- Approval green: `#6EA777`
- Warning red: `#C8644F`

## Typography

- Display: `DM Serif Display`
- UI / body: `IBM Plex Sans`
- Data / receipts: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Motion

- First 1.5 seconds must land as a quote, not a logo.
- Visible state change every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Use focus pulls, warm light leaks, and vertical pushes instead of hard jump cuts.
- Product card appears as physical proof around the midpoint, not as an opening logo.
- Last frame visually rhymes with the first card frame to encourage rewatch.

## What NOT To Do

- No source movie footage or source movie audio.
- No logo-only opening.
- No generic AI orb, robot, neural net, or neon gradient.
- No in-video URL, "link in bio", "join waitlist", or "pre-order now" CTA.
- No explanation that requires knowing AI jargon.
