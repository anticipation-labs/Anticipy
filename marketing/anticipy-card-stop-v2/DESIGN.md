# Anticipy Card Stop V2

## Creative

First-frame interruption, not explanation. The viewer sees the warning before the product. The proof is that the AI hits the money boundary and stops.

## Palette

- Ink: `#090908`
- Paper: `#F4EFE7`
- Cream: `#FFF8EA`
- Red: `#E0523D`
- Acid: `#D9FF5B`
- Brass: `#C8A86A`
- Muted: `#81786A`

## Type

- Heavy sans for warnings and captions.
- Serif only for the final human line.
- No logo card. No polished SaaS hero. No centered empty stacks.

## Motion

- First second: impact, shake, red flash.
- Beat cuts every 1-2 seconds.
- Keep captions burned in and readable without audio.
- End loops visually back to the opening warning.
