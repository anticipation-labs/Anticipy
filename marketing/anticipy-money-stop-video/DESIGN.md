# Anticipy Money Stop Video Design

## Style Prompt

Native vertical short-form proof video for Anticipy. It should feel like a real founder's phone,
checkout receipt, and local assistant trace, not a glossy SaaS ad. The first frame is tension:
payment blocked before anything irreversible. Visual language is charcoal, warm cream, brass,
paper grain, red safety marks, screen glow, and imperfect receipt motion.

## Colors

- Background: `#11100D`
- Surface: `#F4EFE7`
- Ink: `#191713`
- Muted ink: `#766F62`
- Accent brass: `#C8A86A`
- Safe green: `#6EA777`
- Attention red: `#C8644F`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`
- UI / body: `Inter`

## Motion

- First frame is already a receipt, not a brand card.
- Visible state change every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Use wipes and receipt slides instead of generic jump cuts.
- End frame visually rhymes with the opening payment stop.

## What NOT To Do

- No logo before second 10.
- No generic AI orb, robot, neon gradient, or abstract network brain.
- No product explanation before proof.
- No "link in bio" in-video.
- No fake platform logos or readable real brand marks.
