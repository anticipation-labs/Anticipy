# Ten Viral-Proof Video Scripts

These are built as rendered, faceless proof videos. Every video follows the same retention spine: receipt/tension at 0.0s, concrete sentence by 4s, mechanism by 7s, human assistant line by 10s, loopable Anticipy reveal at the end.

## 01. Email I Did Not Write

- Hook: Receipt.
- First frame: `i did not write this.`
- Comment gap: "how did it know Dana?"
- Share trigger: founders sending to founders who forget follow-ups.
- Post copy: `said it once. draft was waiting.`
