# Anticipy Viral Proof Batch Design

## Style Prompt

Premium native short-form proof videos for an ambient AI assistant. The work should feel like a real founder's desk, phone, and browser receipts, not a glossy SaaS ad. First frame is proof or tension, not brand. Visual language is charcoal, warm cream, soft brass, paper grain, screen glow, and subtle imperfect motion.

## Colors

- Background: `#11100D`
- Surface: `#F4EFE7`
- Ink: `#191713`
- Muted ink: `#766F62`
- Accent brass: `#C8A86A`
- Safe green: `#6EA777`
- Attention red: `#C8644F`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`
- UI / body: `Inter`

## Motion

- Quick receipt reveals in the first 1.5 seconds.
- Cut or state change every 2 to 3 seconds.
- No jump cuts without a wipe or card slide.
- End frame visually rhymes with the opening to encourage rewatch.

## What NOT To Do

- No logo before second 8.
- No generic AI orb, robot, neon gradient, or abstract network brain.
- No product explanation before proof.
- No "link in bio" in-video.
- No fake platform logos or readable brand names.
- No religious/Bible imagery. The target is viral short-form proof, not religious content.
