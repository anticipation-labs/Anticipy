# Anticipy Viral Research Gate - 2026-06-22

Status: research and creative gate only. No new videos rendered.

## Hard Truth

There is no honest guarantee of 3-4M views from one clip. The operating target is different:
make every clip strong enough that it deserves a serious distribution test, then ship enough
probes that one breakout becomes plausible instead of magical.

The bar for Anticipy is not "AI productivity app." That lane is dead. The bar is:

> "I said one messy thing out loud. It understood the life-admin hiding inside it, did the
> reversible work, and stopped before money."

If a concept does not prove that in the first 7 seconds, it is not an Anticipy launch clip.

## What The Viral Examples Have In Common

1. They are not feature demos first.
   They are conflict, proof, or scandal first. The product shows up as the resolution.

2. They have an enemy.
   Bland's enemy: cold, bad, robotic service calls. Cluely's enemy: social performance anxiety
   and institutional gatekeeping. Rabbit/Humane's accidental enemy: overpromising hardware.

3. They have a sentence people can repeat.
   "Cheat on everything" is ethically radioactive, but mechanically strong. Anticipy needs a safer
   sentence that carries the same compression.

4. They make the founder/product look a little unsafe to ignore.
   Not polished. Not brand-safe to the point of boredom. The viewer has to think: "wait, are they
   actually doing this?"

5. They create debate.
   The strongest clips invite one-word comments: "creepy", "need", "fake", "finally", "privacy",
   "where".

## Anticipy Position

Working sentence:

> Not a chatbot. A second nervous system for errands.

Sharper launch line:

> It catches the admin hiding inside your day.

Enemy:

- Chatbots that make you type a second job.
- Calendar/task apps that require you to remember the thing first.
- AI hardware that demos magic but cannot handle petty real life.

Buyer emotion:

- "I am drowning in tiny obligations."
- "I do not need motivation. I need the thing handled."
- "I hate asking people for help with small stuff."
- "I need a human assistant, but I cannot afford or manage one."

Preorder reason:

- Early users get the local/private build and setup help.
- Limited onboarding because it needs real connection work.
- The real promise is not a gadget; it is a five-day life-admin trial.

## Creative Rules Before Production

Every Anticipy clip must pass all 12:

1. First frame is a receipt, mistake, or uncomfortable human moment. No logo.
2. Hook is spoken or visible by 0.5s.
3. Product is not explained before 7s.
4. The clip proves one petty admin task, not "AI productivity."
5. One proper noun appears before 4s: Gmail, Aetna, Target, Calendar, Delta, Comcast, school, dentist.
6. One number/time appears before 5s: "$73", "2:47am", "three Tuesdays", "18 unread".
7. There is a hard safety line: no money, no sending, no irreversible action without approval.
8. The CTA is in caption/post copy, not a logo card.
9. The CTA is specific: "comment ADMIN", "DM 'waitlist'", "preorder if this is your day."
10. The last frame loops visually into the first frame.
11. A viewer can send it to one specific person for one specific reason.
12. It has a predicted comment word.

Reject if:

- It says "meet", "introducing", "AI assistant", "revolutionary", "productivity", "unlock".
- It feels like a landing-page hero.
- It requires the viewer to understand Anticipy before they care.
- The only CTA is "link in bio."

## CTA System

Primary CTA:

- "Comment ADMIN" for public posts.
- Auto-reply or manual reply with preorder/onboarding link.

Secondary CTA:

- "DM MEMORY" for higher-intent users who want privacy/setup details.

Founder-account CTA:

- "I am onboarding the first X people manually. If your life looks like this, comment ADMIN."

No CTA:

- No "link in bio" burned into the video.
- No "follow for more."
- No logo end card under 30s.

## 48-Hour Operating Loop

Phase 1: research capture.
- Pull 30 more live examples from TikTok Creative Center, X, Instagram, YouTube Shorts.
- Score them by hook type, first frame, proof object, CTA, comments, share trigger.
- Save only structure, never copy exact creative.

Phase 2: script gate.
- Convert the ten angles above into full 11-part scripts.
- Each script gets a score from 0-100.
- Anything under 85 is killed.

Phase 3: production.
- Produce only the top 3 first.
- Original sound/VO, human imperfections, no corporate polish.
- Track: 3-second hold, completion, rewatch, comments per 1K views, shares per 1K views, preorder clicks.

Phase 4: iteration.
- Within 48h, remake the winner with three hook variants.
- Do not polish losers. Extract the winning mechanic.

## Minimum Confidence Bar Before Render

I will not render another Anticipy marketing video unless it has:

- a named viral precedent,
- a specific first frame,
- a real proof object,
- a caption CTA,
- a share recipient,
- a predicted comment,
- and a reason someone skeptical would still watch to the end.

Anything else is just content-shaped waste.
