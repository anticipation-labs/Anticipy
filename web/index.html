How it works

It works the way a great assistant would — by paying attention.

01

It hears what you actually meant.

You talk the way people talk — venting, joking, trailing off. Anticipy follows the thread and separates the signal from the noise. A sarcastic “oh perfect, another dentist reminder” isn’t a task. “I really need to move that flight” is. It learns the difference, and it remembers what you told it last week.

02

It does the quiet work.

Then it just… handles it. Drafts the email in your voice. Books the table. Fills the form in your own Chrome, signed in as you, the way you would have. When something needs a real call, it can pick up the phone in a voice you genuinely can’t tell from a person — and speak for you.

03

It closes the loop with you.

You stay in the room. Before anything is spent, sent, or made permanent, Anticipy comes back with a plain-language summary and waits for your nod. The work is done in the background; the decision stays yours. You end the day lighter, not managing an app.

The promise

It never spends your money, or your trust, without asking.

Anticipy can do a great deal on its own — but it knows the difference between a chore and a choice. Anything that costs money, sends in your name, or can’t be undone stops and waits for one word from you. Everything it remembers about your life stays yours, private, and revocable. The autonomy is real. So is the brake.
