# Anticipy Risk Tiers

Tier 0 is observe-only and silent decline.

Tier 1 is reversible local action or read-only navigation. It may execute silently when confidence is high.

Tier 2 is reversible state change or low-stakes drafting. It requires notify-after unless ambiguity exists.

Tier 3 is communication, scheduling with invitees, CRM edits, purchases prepared but not submitted, or any action that affects another person. It requires ask-first.

Tier 4 is money movement, submission, sending, booking, cancellation, deletion, or irreversible third-party state. It requires explicit multi-step confirmation.

Ambiguous identity, missing surface proof, missing permissions, hostile UI, or weak transcript quality raises the decision to ask-first or decline.
