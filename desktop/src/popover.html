One macOS pop-up at a time
When you click Allow Microphone below, macOS will pop up its own Allow or Deny window. Click Allow there, then we move on to the next one. We do not collect anything; the prompts come straight from macOS.

Plug Anticipy into Chrome.

Anticipy uses your own Chrome so it can act on the websites you are already signed into. This is the only part that needs a few clicks.

We copied the folder path for you
When you hit Load unpacked in Chrome, paste right away. The path is already on your clipboard. ~/.anticipy/extension/anticipy-v6/EXTENSION-LOAD-THIS-IN-CHROME
  1. 1
    Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
    The big Open Chrome button below does this for you.
  2. 2
    Flip on Developer mode in the top right.
    It is a small toggle next to the search box.
  3. 3
    Click Load unpacked.
    A folder picker opens.
  4. 4
    Paste the path we copied for you, then hit Select.
    Press Cmd+Shift+G in the folder picker, paste, Enter.
