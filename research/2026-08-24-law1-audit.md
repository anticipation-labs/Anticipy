## THE ANSWER, UP FRONT

**The claim is half true, and the half that is false is the half that decides.**

There *is* a real LLM harness. `Brain.triage()` (`brain/orchestrator.py:434`) sends every
line to a model at temperature 0, with the conversation, the previous line, the sticky
addressee, the voice verdict, the meeting posture and recalled memory appended; anything
about to become an act or an ask is re-judged by a **frontier second opinion**
(`orchestrator.py:492-505`) which is allowed to *demote* as well as sharpen. Sufficiency
(`check_sufficiency:1313`), read-into-a-machine (`read_into_a_machine:1107`), same-plan
(`_same_plan:3861`) and SMS intent (`conversation._classify:1228`) are separate model
calls. `read_into_a_machine` is the exemplar of the law working: mechanical evidence is
gathered by regex and handed *to the model as evidence*, and the model still decides.

But that harness is **fenced on both sides by pattern-matching**:

- **Before it.** Seven constructs in `hear()` end the pipeline with a verdict *before any
  model is called* — a briefing router, a memory-question router, a quotation detector, a
  memory-fact detector, a dictation detector, a two-word floor, and a go-ahead phrase list
  that releases a held consequential card. On the phone, *upstream of all of it*, a 0.7
  word-overlap threshold deletes spoken lines so they never reach the brain at all.
- **After it.** At least ten constructs veto, rewrite or discard what the model returned —
  the largest being `inherited_errand()`, a seven-word-list machine that flips act/ask to
  ignore and deletes the goal.
- **Around it.** In the extension and the iOS app — the two organs that actually touch the
  world — there is essentially **no model in the loop for meaning at all**. Consent to read
  the owner's email, cancellation of an errand, whether the human is ever asked a question,
  and whether a page is a paywall are all decided by word lists.

**Zero gate legs in this repository go red because a piece of tape exists.** Three `TAPE:`
comments exist in code; the only leg that mentions tape (`tejas_gate.py` leg 2) asserts the
tape's **presence** and fails if it is removed. `overnight/tejas_gate.py` is **8/8 GREEN**
today — including leg 4, which `HARNESS-LAWS.md` names as the leg tracking `_READ_ONLY_RE`'s
removal, while `_READ_ONLY_RE` is still in the file and still deciding.

## §4. IS THE MODEL ACTUALLY DECIDING?

### Where it genuinely is

- `Brain.triage()` (`orchestrator.py:434`) — one model call per line at `temperature=0.0`, with
  a re-ask on unparseable JSON that **changes the prompt** rather than replaying it (`:456-470`).
- A **frontier second opinion** on anything about to act or ask (`:492-505`), which *replaces*
  the cheap verdict in **either direction** — it may demote a hallucinated act to ignore. It is
  correctly gated on `self.strong.live` so a keyless rig cannot overrule a real model.
- A **second look** on an `ignore` that still names a task (`:568-580`).
- A **channel declaration** — the model names `touches: compute | read | world` and
  `is_consequential` enforces it (`anticipy_core.py:559-598`). This is the law working as
  designed: meaning from the model, enforcement from the gate.
- `check_sufficiency` (`:1313`), `read_into_a_machine` (`:1107`), `_same_plan`'s model arm
  (`:3869`), `_classify` (`conversation.py:1228`), `_about_pending` (`:1212`), `_voice`
  (`anticipy_core.py:2565`), memory `EXTRACT_SYSTEM` / `CONSOLIDATE_SYSTEM` / `SAME_FACT`.

### Thresholds and rules that override it

The `_IRREVERSIBLE_RE` case is worth stating plainly, because it is the one the laws sanction
and it still costs something measurable:

```
is_consequential("check out the reviews for that dentist", touches="read")  -> True   (held)
is_consequential("Check out times for the hotel",          touches="read")  -> True   (held)
is_consequential("Confirm the Earls booking details are right", touches="read") -> True
```
✅ VERIFIED. The model declared `read`. The deny-list holds anyway, because `check out` and
`confirm` are in `_VERBS`. This is fail-closed and I classify it **LEGAL — seatbelt**; but it
is a *wording* test, not an effect-channel test, and Law 1 draws that line explicitly. Three
pure lookups a night, each costing a confirmation tap, is exactly the "teaches him to tap
through prompts without reading" failure the file's own comment warns about.

### Is the model given enough context?

**No. The board's note is confirmed by the code: she judges LINE BY LINE.**

`Anticipy._decide` (`anticipy_core.py:2397-2502`) builds **one prompt per line**. The
conversation is appended as decoration:

```python
if convo:
    earlier = " | ".join(c for c in convo[-16:] if c and c != line)
    prompt = f"{prompt}\n(Earlier in this conversation: {earlier})"
```

The judged **unit** is the line; the segment is a string in a parenthesis. `Brain.triage` takes
a single `transcript_line`. There is no segment-close call anywhere in `brain/`.

Three things compound it:

1. **The 16-line window is chosen by `segmenter.py`'s regex/clock boundaries** (#48), and
   `worker.py:3280-3284` feeds `recent_turns()` straight into the triage prompt — so a word
   count of 8 and a 45-second timer decide what the model may see.
2. **54% of the recorded call's lines were ≤ 4 words** (`tejas_gate` leg 8 pins this, and it
   passes). Judging a 2-word shard with a pipe-joined string of prior lines is what
   `shard_too_thin` exists to survive.
3. `docs/BOARD-STATE-2026-08-24.md:141-152` records the measured consequence:
   *"Judgment measured 68% → 80% on the labeled set. Remaining blindness: she still judges LINE
   BY LINE."* The recommended fix — *"conversation-granularity judging — one strong-model call
   per closed segment … retires the shard tape"* — is listed as **not built**.

### Does a fallback fire when the model is unavailable, and does it pattern-match?

**Yes, in three places, and all three pattern-match.**

1. **`brain/llm.py:396 _heuristic()`** — nine regexes returning `{"decision":"act","goal":
   "find_and_book_restaurant"}` and friends. It is returned by `chat()` (`:285`) whenever no
   `GEMINI_API_KEY`/`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is set. **In fairness to production wiring:**
   `worker.py:3082-3083` passes `llm if llm.live else None` to both `Memory` and `Anticipy`,
   and `Anticipy.__init__:978` only builds a `Brain` when `llm` is truthy — so a keyless worker
   has *no brain at all* rather than a regex brain, and a live key that starts erroring
   **raises** rather than falling back (`llm.py:329, 389`). The heuristic is therefore a **rig**,
   which is why I classify it LEGAL — gate/eval. Two caveats stand: `Brain.__init__` at
   `orchestrator.py:396` does `self.llm = llm or LLM()`, so any direct construction gets it;
   and `overnight/triage_eval.py` **measures it by default** (`--live` is opt-in), which means
   an offline "triage score" is a score of nine regexes, not of the harness. The user memory
   note "triage_eval needs `--live` or it silently measures nothing" is exactly this.
2. **`brain/conversation.py:1254-1278`** — the SMS confirm/decline classifier (#32). This one
   is **not** an outage path: it runs on any malformed model reply and can release or cancel a
   held job.
3. **`brain/memory.py:1313 _rule_extract`** — `_COMMIT_RE` + `_NAME_RE` + a topic keyword list,
   reached when `llm` is None **and when the live model raises** (`:1257-1263` prints and falls
   through). It assigns the promise to `people[0]` — whichever capitalised word came first.
4. And the deepest one: `Anticipy._decide`'s offline arm — `if mem.get("commitment"): return
   Decision(decision="act", goal="agent_goal", …)`.

## §5. VERDICT ON THE OWNER'S CLAIM

> *"The product uses an LLM-based harness (not simple keyword matching) to recognize tasks
> from voice input."*

**Where it is true.** The core judgment — is this a task, whose is it, who was it addressed
to, what channel does it touch, what is missing — is made by a model, at temperature 0, with
conversation context, worked examples, a voice verdict, a meeting posture and a frontier
second opinion on anything about to act. That is a real harness, and `read_into_a_machine` and
the `touches` declaration are genuinely well-built instances of the law it is meant to obey.

**Where it is not true.**

1. **Not for every sentence.** On the phone, a 0.7 word-overlap threshold
   (`TranscriptFlushPolicy.swift:61`) deletes lines before they are uploaded, and a word list
   (`AnticipyApp.swift:1446`) cancels errands before they are sent. For those sentences there is
   no harness at all, LLM-based or otherwise.
2. **Not at the front door.** Seven constructs in `hear()` return a final verdict without ever
   calling a model: the briefing router, the memory-question router, the quotation detector,
   the memory-fact detector, the dictation detector, the two-word floor, and the go-ahead
   phrase list — the last of which releases a held consequential card.
3. **Not at the back door.** Thirteen places veto, rewrite or replace what the model returned.
   The largest, `inherited_errand`, is a seven-word-list machine that deletes both the verdict
   and the goal. `conversation.py:1511` throws out a frontier model's answer on
   `set(re.findall(r"[a-z]{4,}", …))`.
4. **Not in the hands or the eyes.** In `extension/` and `app/ios/` — the organs that touch the
   world — meaning is decided by keyword matching almost everywhere: consent to read email,
   whether the human is asked a question, what a form field means, whether a page is a paywall,
   whether a checkbox was approved. **24 violations across those two directories, zero `TAPE:`
   comments.**
5. **Not with the context to decide.** The model is handed one line with a pipe-joined string of
   neighbours, and which neighbours it gets is decided by an 8-word count and a 45-second timer
   in `segmenter.py`. The board's own number — 68% → 80% — is the ceiling of line-by-line
   judging, and the fix that removes it is not built.
6. **Not under Law 2.** Three `TAPE:` comments exist, none has a gate leg that goes red because
   the tape is present, one names a leg that tests something else, one names no leg at all, and
   the leg that does mention tape **fails if the tape is removed**. `tejas_gate` is 8/8 green
   with every piece of tape still in the tree.

**Plain answer: the harness is LLM-based at its centre and keyword-based at every edge that
touches the owner or the world.** The claim as stated would be defensible about
`Brain.triage()` alone. It is not defensible about the product, because a sentence has to
survive an iOS similarity threshold, a phone-side cancellation word list, seven pre-model
routers, twelve post-model vetoes, and an extension whose every meaning decision is a regex,
before it becomes something the owner asked for.

**The single worst item:** `extension/side_trip.js:205-228` — a word list decides that the
owner consented to having his email read, in a file whose own comment says *"A false positive
here reads somebody's mail without being asked."*

**The most structurally damaging item:** `overnight/tejas_gate.py` leg 2 — the only gate leg
in the repository that mentions tape asserts the tape's **presence**. As long as that is the
enforcement mechanism, Law 2 cannot catch anything, and the three-month loop the laws were
written to end has no brake on it.
