The manufacturer TDS identifies TA4550 as a two-part structural acrylic for
difficult plastics, lists nylon/polyamide and PETG performance, and specifies
approximately 5-6 minutes fixture time, about two hours to working strength,
and 24 hours to full cure at 23 C. The TDS was visually reviewed in full.

TA4550 is the documented target, not an authorization to use an unknown or
expired tube. Confirm the exact shell material, adhesive lot/expiry, local
stock, surface process, room temperature, coupon result, and complete cure as
`ADHESIVE_AND_FINISH_GATE.md` requires.
