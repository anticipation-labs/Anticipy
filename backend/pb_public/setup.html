Anticipy

Give Anticipy hands.

Five minutes, once. After this, Anticipy can do real work in your Chrome — always asking you first.

Step 1 — Download Download the browser agent and unzip it (double-click the file).
Download Anticipy for Chrome
Step 2 — Open Chrome's extensions page In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
Step 3 — Turn on Developer mode Flip the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner of that page.
Step 4 — Load Anticipy Click Load unpacked (top-left) and choose the unzipped anticipy-extension folder.
Step 5 — Pair with your phone A welcome tab opens with a 6-digit pair code. In the Anticipy app on your phone, open Settings and type that code. Done — the app will show Agent live.

Anticipy never touches banking sites, never fills passwords or payment fields, and always stops at logins and CAPTCHAs. Every consequential action waits for your OK first.
