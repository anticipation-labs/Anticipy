ANTICIPY

Privacy, in plain language.

Anticipy listens for work you want done, remembers what you tell it, and—only after you approve a task—can carry out that exact task in your Chrome. That requires personal data. This page says exactly what moves and why.

Last updated August 12, 2026

The short version

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, track you across apps or websites, or run third-party analytics. We use your data to operate Anticipy for you. You can ask us to delete the server copy at any time.

What the iPhone app handles

  • Your account and profile: email, phone number, name, birthday if you add it, timezone, and facts you choose to tell Anticipy.
  • What Anticipy hears: the finalized text transcript is sent to Anticipy's backend. When on-device speech recognition is available, raw microphone audio stays on the phone. Otherwise iOS may send audio to Apple's speech-recognition service to produce that text. If you use an Anticipy pendant, its Opus audio is sent to Deepgram for live transcription; the finalized text then follows the same Anticipy backend path.
  • Work and memory: requests, approvals, task state, results, and the text memories needed to answer later questions.

What the Chrome extension handles

  • Pairing and liveness: a private browser identity, extension version, browser user-agent, pairing state, and last-seen time go to the Anticipy backend.
  • During an approved task: the task goal, a structured map of the working page, and a downscaled screenshot are sent to OpenRouter so the selected model can choose the next browser action. Profile facts such as your name, email, phone, birthday, address, or other saved facts are included only when the approved task needs them.
  • Proof and recovery: the backend stores the job state, exact approved facts, result, and evidence needed to prevent a guessed completion or an unsafe automatic retry.

The extension also keeps its pairing identity, an opaque backend model-routing marker, current task, and owned tab identifiers in Chrome's local extension storage on that computer.

Services that process the data

  • Railway hosts Anticipy's backend and encrypted service configuration.
  • OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider process the task context, page map, screenshots, and relevant profile facts needed to reason about a task.
  • Twilio carries text messages between Anticipy and your phone.
  • Apple speech recognition may process microphone audio only when this iPhone cannot perform the recognition on-device.
  • Deepgram processes live audio from a paired Anticipy pendant to produce transcript text. The app receives a short-lived access token; the long-lived Deepgram key remains on Anticipy's backend.

These processors receive data only to deliver their part of Anticipy. Their own retention and security terms also apply to the data they process.

How long it stays

Account, transcript, memory, agent, job, and receipt records remain in the Anticipy backend while your account is active so the product can remember, recover, and prove what happened. The extension's local data remains until it is cleared or the extension is removed. There is not yet an automatic expiry for these records.

When you request deletion, we delete the data Anticipy controls from the active backend and disconnect paired browser agents. Provider copies and security backups may take additional time to expire under the provider's retention rules.

Your choices

  • Turn listening off whenever you do not want Anticipy to hear.
  • Do not approve a browser task; Anticipy will not start it.
  • Use Forget me on this phone to clear local app identity and unsent text. That does not by itself delete the server copy.
  • Email us to access, correct, or delete the data Anticipy controls.

Contact and deletion

Email hello@anticipationlabs.com. For deletion, use the subject Anticipy — please delete my data and include the Anticipy ID shown in the app's Settings screen. We will confirm receipt and write back when the deletion is complete.
