Bring Anticipy to your Mac.

Capture both sides of meetings, transcribe them on your Mac, and keep the recording under your control.

Download for Mac

Send this page to the Mac where you take meetings.

Choose AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or another share option.

  1. Install the app

    Download the ZIP, open it, then move AnticipyMac to Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

    Download Anticipy for Mac

  2. Allow the three things it needs

    macOS asks once. Anticipy cannot hear a complete meeting without all three.

    MicrophoneYour side
    System AudioEveryone else
    Speech RecognitionOn-device transcript

  3. Sign in and record

    Use the same Anticipy account as your iPhone. Start and stop from the menu-bar icon; nothing records until you start it.

Your audio stays local. Anticipy saves separate microphone and meeting-audio tracks on your Mac. Only transcript text syncs to your Anticipy account when you are signed in.
