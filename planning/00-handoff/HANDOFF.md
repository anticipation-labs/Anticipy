# Anticipy planning handoff

Working doc. Owner: Omar. Drafted 2026-05-29.

## What Anticipy IS

An AI pendant that listens to everything around you and silently completes the things people ask you to do (and the things you ask yourself out loud). Donna from Suits, for everyone. The pendant is the always-on capture. The phone is the edge brain. A mini-PC (Raspberry-Pi-class, ~$30) runs Chrome and acts. The Mac app today is the prototype of that final form, built so the engine ports unchanged to the mini-PC later.

Latency target for the smallest actions: 1-3 seconds from "wait, when did the Roman empire fall" to the answer arriving in your ear or on your phone. Bigger actions (draft motion, schedule labs, send the quote) finish quietly within the natural window of "before you next think about it." The point is loop closure without you ever opening the app.

## Use case range (no industry-specific build)

Same code path serves:
- Student in lecture, assignment captured + drafted + submission-ready before they leave the room
- Lawyer at intake, demand letter drafted in matter system before the client's car pulls out
- Doctor on rounds, lab orders + consult routed in Epic before the next room
- Construction PM, schedule + city portal + sub emails updated from a 5-second conversation
- Sales rep on discovery, CRM + CPQ quote + Slack-to-SE drafted before the next call
- Trivia debate with a friend, answer arrives in 1.2 seconds
- Spouse asks for Italian reservation + flowers Saturday, OpenTable + florist done

The pattern is identical: noisy utterance → resolve implicit context (who, what software, what template, what authority) → execute in real software → don't screw up → stay quiet.

## Components and their cold-start dependencies

| Component | What it does | Cold-start dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Pendant mic → ASR (parakeet on edge today, mini model on phone later) | None, works on day 1 |
| Memory | Who are the people, projects, tools, templates in your life | EVERYTHING. This is the hard part. |
| Planner | Utterance + memory → "do this thing in this app" | Memory + good model |
| Surface adapter | Actually drive Chrome (CDP) or native macOS | App-specific recipes |
| Confidence ladder | silent / notify / confirm / refuse | Per-action calibration that improves with use |
| Notification surface | Pendant haptic + earbud audio + phone notification, never interrupt | UX design |
| Authority vault | We are logged into your stuff, persistently, across MFA + session expiry | Real per-app integration work |

## How we work from here

- Planning folder is the source of truth. Every component decision lives here before any code change.
- Each thread folder has an OPTIONS.md (the 3-7 ways to solve it), RECOMMENDED.md (the chosen approach + why), and OPEN.md (what's still unanswered).
- When we commit to an approach, the code change happens, and the relevant planning doc gets a "shipped in commit XXX" note at the top.
- No more gate-chasing. No more "all green" claims without a real-user-flow proof.
- Agents work in parallel and write into their assigned thread folder. They do not edit each other's folders.

## Cold-start summary

Seven strategies cover the day-0 inhale, sequenced over the first week and all writing into the existing `UserProfile` seam at `engine/app/anticipy/seams.py` so nothing downstream changes. (A) Browser-tab inhale via the `<all_urls>` extension at `extension_v4/manifest.json` plus CDP at `engine/app/action_engine/cdp_dispatcher.py`, walking `Target.getTargets` and attaching `Page.enable + Runtime.evaluate` for per-surface recipes against Gmail, Calendar, Drive, LinkedIn, Slack, and the long tail of B2B SaaS. (B) Twilio outbound call using the wired `engine/app/anticipy/comms.py` channel to run the existing seven-question `INTERVIEW_SCRIPT` in `engine/app/anticipy/onboarding.py`. (C) Filesystem scan via `mdfind` for templates, naming conventions, and writing voice from `~/Documents`. (D) Calendar-graph relationship inference that fills `UserProfile.people` from attendee cadence. (E) Role-template priors from `roles/` matched on a single "what do you do" answer, marked low-provenance so observations overwrite them. (F) Federated CSV/JSON dump import for the power-user 5%. (G) Passive 72-hour ambient listening via the existing `engine/app/proactive/` cascade, gated by the conservative ACT threshold in `engine/app/coldstart/ramp.py`. The 5-minute MVP is E plus A's Gmail-signature and 7-day-calendar inhales, sufficient to resolve "remind me to prep for the Sarah meeting tomorrow" and write to draft on day 0. Open problems: MDM-locked Chrome that blocks both extension and CDP, native EHRs (Epic Hyperdrive, Clio) outside the browser, first-action regret window, voice-anchor binding for non-wearer speakers, cross-device dossier merge, and the post-job-change privacy reset. Full doc at `planning/01-cold-start/OPTIONS.md`.

## Cross-app-auth summary

Current surface is the CDP bridge at `scripts/v7/anticipy_bridge_fallback_cdp.py` + `engine/app/action_engine/cdp_dispatcher.py`, talking to the user's real Chrome on `:9222` (launched with `--remote-debugging-port=9222` against the real user-data-dir, not a clone). Cookies and sessions for already-logged-in apps come for free. Today's gaps: no tab-ownership tagging so the agent can hijack the user's Gmail tab via in-place reuse; no per-app session-refresh daemon; Twilio MFA scaffold exists in `engine/app/product/login_wall_responder.py` but only handles "type your password," not 6-digit codes. Recommended path is a hybrid: default to the user's real Chrome with an "Anticipy" tab group (extension already implements this in `extension_v4/background.js:504-517`), spawn isolated per-app contexts only for sensitive auth steps and roll captured cookies back. MFA via Twilio `<Gather speech>` for SMS/push codes, opt-in local TOTP seed for authenticator codes, iMessage fallback for users in meetings. CAPTCHA stack at `engine/app/captcha.py` is fine; needs NopeCHA bundled into installer. Bot-detection refusal list (Ticketmaster, Kasada-protected) explicit. Per-app config registry under `engine/config/auth_profiles/<app>.json` is the unit of "Anticipy supports this SaaS." Open: per-org Salesforce/Epic subdomain matching, banks (probably out of scope V1), HIPAA review for Epic, native EHRs that are not browser-based.

## Quietness-UX summary

The cascade skeleton already exists. `engine/app/proactive/` ships the NOTED/IN_APP/PUSH/SMS/VOICE channel ladder, an LLM urgency 1-5 scorer that maps to channel, a Donna refuse layer, an EXECUTE/ASK/LOG/REFUSE decider, a fyi cap at PUSH, and an LLM-driven dispatcher dedup gate. `engine/app/proactive_day/comms.py` has the older `silent_queue` plus debounce-and-compose merging that we should port forward. `engine/app/proactive_day/timing.py` already understands "after the meeting" against `world.calendar`. What is missing: real device-side delivery callables (DeliveryRoutes are all empty in prod wiring), a live calendar adapter, an ambient-meeting detector that reuses the existing diarization stream, pendant haptic/LED protocol, earbud TTS, and the between-meetings digest renderer. The proposal: four notification classes mapped to surfaces (silent-done → NOTED + slow gold LED pulse; notify-after → IN_APP + double pulse; confirm-before → haptic + LED amber + earbud TTS during quiet windows, queued during busy ones; ask-before → never interrupts a meeting without a meeting-internal deadline). Quietness is the union of calendar busy windows, ambient-meeting detection (>40% non-wearer airtime in last 90s), and user-toggle DND. The trivia path has a 1500ms p50 budget broken down into BLE in 80ms + Parakeet ASR finalize 220ms + on-device salience/intent 230ms + cloud lookup 600ms + TTS start 100ms + BLE out 80ms + overhead, with phone push parallel. Confirmation is pendant double-tap for yes / swipe-down for no / 30-second default-deny on confirm-class. Failure-mode floor: if the user is completely unreachable and a money-irreversible action has a <2-minute deadline, fire one phone vibrate via Apple Critical Alerts entitlement (requires App Review approval, listed as open problem). Full doc at `planning/04-quietness-ux/DESIGN.md`.

## Confidence-ladder summary

Today's decision surface is split across three files that don't share state: `engine/app/product/risk_assessor.py:185` (returns one of `silent | notify | confirm | ask`, no `refuse` mode per the never-decline directive), `engine/app/product/confirm_card.py:242` (binary `needs_confirmation`, what the dispatcher actually keys off of), and `engine/app/safety.py:104` (`ALWAYS_BLOCKED`/`ALWAYS_CONFIRM` keyword tables). Calibration is hardcoded verb tables and irreversibility weights at `risk_assessor.py:17-171`. The only learned input is `scoped_memory.is_do_not_touch()` at `scoped_memory.py:290`; `relationship_sensitive` is read but never written. Proposal: rename to a clean 4-class taxonomy (silent / notify / confirm / refuse), introduce `refuse` for password-typing / money-transfer / market-orders / legal-name signatures that hands back to a prefilled surface, add double-tap confirm for medical/legal/financial-irreversible surfaces (Epic, Clio, court e-filing, `*.gov`). Per-user calibration runs locally on three signals: reversal within N minutes, edit-before-send diff, manual redo within 24 h. Scores per (surface, recipient_class, verb) tuple in [0,1] with hysteresis (0.75 up to drop a rung, 0.40 down to climb), stored as new `KIND_CALIBRATION` items in the existing `~/.anticipy/v7/memory/<account_id>/<device_id>/memory.jsonl`. First-week dial is action-count-based not calendar-based: 0-30 actions = confirm everything third-party, 30-150 = notify on low-risk externals/confirm on sends, 150+ = per-user calibration takes over. Hard rule: every silent action must produce a `ReversalLog` entry with a revert recipe (delete draft, cancel booking, restore calendar snapshot, etc.); if no revert recipe exists, the action is not eligible for silent. Cross-class transactions: if any primitive is `confirm`/`refuse` the whole bundle goes to `confirm`; partial-failure offers reversal for already-run silent steps. Open: per-recipient class resolver (need a `PersonClassifier` beyond `person_resolver.py`), reversal-recipe library for the ~30-40 day-1 surfaces, cross-device calibration sync without a server, "didn't reverse but unhappy" signals, first-bad-action weighting, pendant double-tap when phone is across the room, recovery from a wrong calibration via apology-pattern detection. Estimated 1.5 weeks for classifier + reversal log + 30 surface recipes, multi-month calibration in the wild. Full doc at `planning/02-confidence-ladder/DESIGN.md`.

## Competitive-landscape summary

Twelve-competitor scan in `planning/06-competitive-landscape/COMPETITORS.md` (5,700 words). The headline finding: the capture half of the pendant problem is a commodity in May 2026 (Plaud is the most polished, Limitless was the best until Meta acquired it December 2025, Bee was acquired by Amazon July 2025, Humane sold to HP February 2025 and shut down servers, Friend exists only as a parasocial AI-companion novelty), but the action half is unclaimed because Rabbit's LAM was vaporware, Adept ACT-1 never shipped (acqui-hired by Amazon), Multion pivoted to early-access Android (now "AGI Inc."), and Granola/Cogram/Notion AI/Copilot all stop at "draft an email, surface an action item, here's a summary" without executing inside the user's real CRM/EHR/Procore/etc. The cold-start half is also unclaimed: every ambient-capture product on the market today expects the user to wait two-plus weeks before diarization and person-recognition stabilize, and no product delivers a useful <5-minute onboarding from inputs the user already has (Gmail OAuth, calendar, contacts, recent docs, screen recording). Specific salvageable patterns: Granola's no-bot capture, Limitless's calendar auto-link, Plaud's user-pick-the-model transparency, Cogram's vertical-template-plus-system-of-record execution shape, Notion Agent's credit pricing, and AGI Inc.'s on-device Qualcomm bet. Anti-patterns to refuse: always-on capture with no salience filter (data sludge), push-button-only intentional recording (universal "I forgot" regret), trying to replace the smartphone (Humane and Rabbit both died on this hill), subscription-required core functionality, acquisition-shaped business models, and marketing the device as a friend. Anticipy's window is roughly the 18-24 months before Meta ships a Limitless-derived product bundled free with Quest or Ray-Ban Meta, and the work is to make our cold-start and execution halves so much better that "$199 + privacy + actually works" beats "free + Meta."

## Trivia-fire summary

The trivia-fire moment (Roman Empire debate, 1.2 seconds to answer) is specified in `planning/07-trivia-fire/DESIGN.md`. Trigger is a four-feature classifier (lexical opener n-grams, prosodic question contour, group-conversation context, recent-answer absence) tuned for 90%+ precision over 50% recall because misfires in front of a friend are the dominant cost. Latency budget closes at 1720 ms via parakeet_mlx streaming finalize (300 ms), a 5 to 8 MB on-phone distilBERT intent classifier (100 ms), a three-lane answer router with a ~120 MB local Wikidata cache covering an estimated 35 to 45% of questions (5 to 15 ms) falling through to Perplexity Sonar Small Online (700 ms p50) or Brave + Sonnet (~900 ms backup), and a direct local BLE + APNs path that bypasses the model broker (200 ms). Surfaces are pendant haptic ack at t=0, earbud TTS if connected, phone notification with the answer as the title for lock-screen glance, and Mac menubar fallback. Fire gated at confidence >= 0.85 (estimated 1 misfire per 20 user-days at 5 fires/day), with per-friend-group threshold calibration learned from voice-print-hashed speaker IDs and explicit dismiss/thumbs-down signals. Anti-spam via topic-embedding dedup (30s window), hard cap 1 fire/min, daily soft cap 20. Open risks: parakeet streaming finalize on phone-class hardware, Perplexity p99 spikes, two-party-consent legal exposure (CA/FL/MA), lecture-mode silent queue, stale-fact cache freshness. Killer demo is a 30 second single-take couch scene ending with Omar tapping the pendant and saying "I just do."
